About

I am an Associate Professor at the Polytechnic of Milan, School of Management and a Senior Affiliated Researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Innovation and Competition in Munich.

My research is in the economics of science and innovation. I study how scientific and technological knowledge is produced and diffused. In recent work, I have examined how funding, international mobility, and research infrastructure shape scientists’ productivity and careers; how firms engage with and learn from science, from participation in scientific conferences to the disclosure of their own research; and how scientific advances drove the evolution of artificial intelligence — and the value of the companies built on it.

I received my PhD in Economics and Management of Innovation from EPFL in 2015. Before joining the Polytechnic of Milan, I was a Assistant Professor at the University of Bath School of Management, a post-doctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute in Munich, and — earlier — a policy analyst in the Science, Technology and Innovation division of the OECD in Paris, where I worked on innovation, inequality, and inclusive-growth policy. I hold a Master and a Bachelor in Management and Industrial Engineering from the Politecnico di Milano.
